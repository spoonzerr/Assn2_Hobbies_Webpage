      Terms

      Baking Dictionary

      The words recipes use without ever explaining.

      Cream
      Beating butter and sugar together until pale, fluffy, and increased in volume.
      Fold
      Gently combining a light mixture into a heavier one without knocking the air out.
      Knead
      Working dough by hand or machine to develop the gluten strength that gives bread its structure.
      Proof
      Letting yeasted dough rest so it has time to rise before baking.
      Roux
      A cooked paste of butter and flour, used as the base for a thickened sauce.
      Score
      Cutting shallow slashes into dough before baking to control where and how it expands.
      Dock
      Pricking small holes into dough so it doesn't puff up unevenly in the oven.
      Rest
      Letting dough or batter sit undisturbed so gluten relaxes or flavour has time to develop.
      Worth the Money

      The Kit That Actually Matters

      Not a full shopping list — just what's genuinely made the biggest difference.

      A digital scale

      Cups lie, grams don't. This single swap fixes more failed bakes than anything else on this list.

      An oven thermometer

      Most home ovens run 15-25°C off from what the dial claims. Yours is probably lying to you right now.

      A cheap bench scraper

      Portions dough, cleans the counter, and lifts sticky things without tearing them. Used more than any knife.

      A wire cooling rack

      Air needs to move under a bake or the bottom steams itself soggy. One rack, endless use.

      A stand or hand mixer

      Not essential on day one, but the moment you try whipping egg whites by hand you'll understand why it's on this list.

      Baking dishes, a few sizes

      For everything savoury on this page — mac and cheese, baked rice, pizza — a couple of reliable dishes cover most of it.
